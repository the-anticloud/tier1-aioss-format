## What We Are Building

The Anticloud is the first sovereign AI infrastructure stack with end-to-end cryptographic audit from sensor to inference. 9 tiers, 194 components, single binary deployment, zero cloud dependency.

**Core claim:** any organization can deploy production-grade AI inference on their own hardware in under an hour, with GDPR/HIPAA-compliant audit logs, fully verified by open-source tooling.

This is not a wrapper around OpenAI. It is the infrastructure that makes cloud AI unnecessary.
